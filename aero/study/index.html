How it was worked out

The airfoil is a Kulfan (CST) shape: eight weights on each surface, a leading-edge weight and the 0.8 mm trailing edge the printer needs. NeuralFoil, a neural network trained on tens of millions of XFOIL analyses, gives its lift, drag, pitching moment, transition points and boundary layer in about a millisecond. SLSQP minimizes the weighted profile drag at the three operating points while holding every limit in the table under the shape chart, and it only accepts shapes NeuralFoil is confident about.

For the whole plane, the wing's profile drag comes from NeuralFoil at each speed, the induced drag from the lift and the aspect ratio (span efficiency 0.8), and every other part from the drag build-up in aero/models/airframe.py. The detail changes are the ones the drag optimizer picks: they are not in the CAD yet.
