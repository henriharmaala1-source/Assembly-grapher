Design efficiency = 3 s x theoretical minimum parts / actual assembly time. Handling and inserting parts is only a fifth of the assembly time; gluing, soldering, taping and bending take the rest, so the biggest wins remove joints, not just parts.

A part counts toward the theoretical minimum when it moves relative to the parts already assembled, must be a different material, or must be separate for assembly or service.
