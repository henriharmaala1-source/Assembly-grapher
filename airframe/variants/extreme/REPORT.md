## The boom

One carbon tube carries the tail, the motor and the prop (31 g on its end), 240 mm out from the fuselage's socket. The first bending mode should stay near the twin booms' (about 49 Hz with the tail alone) and far below the motor (377 rev/s at full throttle). Roll-wrapped carbon (E 70 GPa); a pultruded tube is stiffer in bending but weak in twist and splits in a crash.
