How it was worked out

Both planes are built by airframe/build.py. The optimized one uses the airfoil from the efficiency study and the detail changes its drag optimizer picked, each drawn as a printable part. The build weighs every printed part from its volume (shell and infill for its material) and adds the bought parts. It then moves the wing until the battery balances the plane at 28 % chord, in the middle of its travel.

Each plane flies at its own CAD weight with its own aerodynamics. The wing's profile drag comes from NeuralFoil with its airfoil, at the lift coefficient the weight needs. Every other part comes from the drag build-up with its details, and the induced drag follows the lift. The maximum lift scales with each airfoil's section maximum. Both have the same motor and prop, so the thrust is the same: 1:1 static for the as-built plane, falling to zero at the prop's pitch speed. Power is drag × speed ÷ 40 % propulsion efficiency, plus the avionics; flight time uses 80 % of the 450 mAh pack.

python3 airframe/build.py                       # the as-built CAD
python3 airframe/build.py --variant optimized   # the optimized CAD (airframe/variants/optimized/)
python3 aero/variants.py                        # weights and flight: airframe/variants/compare.json and COMPARE.md
