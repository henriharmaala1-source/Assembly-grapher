# Kipinä single boom, 1:2 desk model: build report

Generated by `mini.py --scale 0.5 --fit 0.3 --gap 0.25` from the single boom's CAD. Do not edit by hand.

A scale model to print: to check how the parts go together before printing the full-size plane, and to show it. It doesn't fly. The parts are the full-size plane's, split the same way and scaled; the bought parts are printed dummies, and the carbon boom and spars are printed rods.

## Fits and tolerances

Scaling halves every clearance the full-size plane has, and a printed part fits a printed part less well than a carbon rod fits a printed hole. So every fit is cut again for the model:

| fit | where | clearance | how |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rod in its hole | boom in the fuselage, tail mount and motor cap; spars in the wing | **0.3 mm** on the diameter | holes drilled for the rod + 0.3 mm; every hole is vertical on the bed, so it prints round |
| Slip fit | lid in the fuselage, fin in the tail mount, the fuselage halves' joint | **0.25 mm** a side (at least 0.17 mm at edges and corners) | the inner part trimmed where it came closer to the outer one |
| Dummy in its pocket | servos, FC, ESC, receiver, VTX, camera, battery | **0.25 mm** a side (measured: at least 0.14 mm) | each dummy trimmed where it came closer to a printed part |
| Fuselage in the stand's cradle | under the balance point | 0.25 mm | the cradle cut by the fuselage's section grown by the gap |
| Glued joints | wing panels on the centre, stabiliser under the tail mount, wing on the fuselage, fairings under the wing | 0 (faces touch) | as designed; nothing overlaps |

**Print `stl/fit_test.stl` first** (33 min, with the same settings as the model). It has a hole for each rod at 0.2, 0.3 and 0.4 mm (one, two and three dots), slots for the 1 mm tail plates at 0.15, 0.25 and 0.35 mm a side, and a stub of each rod and a tail-plate tab to try in them:

- The stubs and the tab slide into the two-dot holes and slots by hand, without play: print the model as it is.
- They only go into the three-dot ones: run `python3 airframe/mini.py --fit 0.4 --gap 0.35`.
- They are loose even in the one-dot ones: `--fit 0.2 --gap 0.15`.

## Assembly

In the full-size plane's order ([BOP.md](../single-boom/BOP.md)), with glue in place of the screws and velcro:

1. Glue the two fuselage halves. Slide the boom into its socket and glue it; glue the motor mount on its end and the motor dummy on the mount, then the prop.
2. Slide the tail mount on the boom and glue the stabiliser under it and the fin into its socket. Glue the elevator and the rudder at neutral.
3. Drop the servo, ESC, FC, receiver, VTX and camera dummies into their places in the fuselage; the battery sits in its bay; put on the lid and the antenna.
4. Push the main and rear spar halves in from each wing tip, through the panel into the wing centre, until they meet in the middle. Put the aileron servo dummies in their pockets, glue the fairings over them and the ailerons on.
5. Seat the wing on the fuselage and stand the plane in the stand's cradle.

What it shows: every printed part's fit against its neighbours, the spar and boom paths, the pockets and bays for the bought parts, and the plane's look. What it can't show: the wiring (left off), the pushrods' travel, the full-size print tolerances (the clearances here are re-cut for the model), and the strength.
