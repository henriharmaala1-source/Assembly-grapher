## Model quirks worth knowing

- **Lower plate, rear 30.5 mm pattern is not square or centred.** These holes have no mirror partner across the centreline: H14 (15.50, -69.50), H20 (15.50, -99.50). The other side's holes are at (-15.00, -69.00), (-15.00, -99.50). A true 30.5 × 30.5 pattern on the centreline would put them at x ±15.25. They are drawn as modelled; check them against your VTX or plate before you rely on them.
- **Wheelbase.** The motor centres are at x ±173.36, y 119.42 (front) and y -157.59 (rear): 346.7 mm apart side to side, 277.0 mm front to back, **443.8 mm** diagonally. RJX lists its Mark4 V2 10" at 427 mm and GEPRC its Mark4 10" at 428-429 mm, so the arms in this model are longer than either. The centre plates and standoffs don't depend on it.
- **Thicknesses.** The model has arms 7 mm, lower and belly plates 3 mm, top plate 2 mm, braces 2 mm, camera plates 2.5 mm. RJX lists 7.5 mm arms; measure yours.
