Kestrel Control Playground

Put the crosshair where the quad should go and set the throttle. It flies along the line through the crosshair, so a crosshair low in the view takes it down and one to the side turns it. Pin the crosshair to a lock to fly relative to an object, record the moves as segments, and copy the mission text to compile with kestrel.
