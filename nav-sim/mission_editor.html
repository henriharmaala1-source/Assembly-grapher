Kestrel Mission Editor

Build the aircraft's behaviour as states. A trigger switches state the moment it holds, for example when the detector sees a door. The steps are what the state does, in order. Copy the text and compile it on your computer; the aircraft only ever gets the compiled file.

Click a step to add it to the selected state, or drag it onto any state.
