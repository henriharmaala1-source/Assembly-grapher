# Tracing the "MK4 Knight" to CADDX's 415 mm frame

No shop, factory or forum in English, Chinese, Ukrainian, Russian or Polish sells a frame called "MK4 Knight" or "Mark4 Knight". The only 10-inch "Knight" frame on the market is the **"CADDX Knight 10" V2 (8mm)"**, a carbon X-frame sold mainly by the Ukrainian shop BraveTech. Its listing gives **8 mm arms, a 415 mm wheelbase, a 2 mm top plate, a 3 mm bottom plate, about 35 mm internal height, 20×20 and 30.5×30.5 mm stack mounts and 16–19 mm motor holes**, at **1250 UAH (about US$30)** ([BraveTech](https://thebravetech.com/product/frame-caddx-knight-10-v2-8mm/)). The 8 mm arm matches your suspicion and sets it apart from every catalogue Mark4 10-inch, which ship with 7 or 7.5 mm arms. Nothing else about your frame can be checked against it. No source publishes the Knight's standoff positions, pair spacings, arm-bolt holes, outline or bare weight, and no CAD file or usable photo was found. The 415 mm wheelbase is also 12–13 mm shorter than the 427 mm Mark4 V2 and 428 mm GEPRC Mark4 10, so if the Knight is a Mark4 at all, it is a resized one rather than a straight clone. One tape-measure reading settles the question: a motor-to-motor diagonal of about 415 mm points to the Knight, and 427–428 mm points to a Mark4 clone sold under another name. **Caveat on the data: every page fetch in this research was blocked by the session's network proxy (DNS failures and HTTP 403 on CONNECT). All figures below come from search-engine summaries of the cited pages and must be checked in a browser before you buy, cut or model anything.**

## The name leads to one Ukrainian-market frame, not a Mark4 product

Exact-phrase searches for "MK4 Knight", "Mark4 Knight", "MK4-Knight" and "Mark 4 Knight", plus the Chinese 骑士 MK4 and the Ukrainian and Russian words for knight (Лицар, Рыцарь), returned no FPV frame. They returned only generic Mark4 10-inch listings and unrelated products ([FlyTeam Mark4 10 V2](https://flyteam.com.ua/frame/mark410v2); [Zhihu Mark4 guide](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/138535933)). The Chinese "Knight" frames that do exist are small and unrelated. 虫骑士 ("Bug Knight") is a 2-inch open-source frame ([MakerWorld China](https://makerworld.com.cn/zh/models/1279667-chong-qi-shi-chuan-yue-ji-ji-cang?from=search)), and **BETAFPV's X-Knight** is a 3–5-inch toothpick line with 140–200 mm wheelbases ([BETAFPV](https://betafpv.com/products/x-knight-4inch-fpv-racing-carbon-fiber-frame-kit)). "MK4 Knight" therefore reads as a seller's or user's shorthand for **"CADDX Knight 10"**, with "MK4" added because Mark4 is the generic name for 10-inch X-frames in Ukrainian and Russian retail. No listing found makes that link, so it remains an inference.

The maker is uncertain. The shop title says "CADDX", but no Caddx product page, manual or catalogue entry for a Knight frame turned up, and Caddx is known for cameras and video systems rather than frames ([search result for Caddx frames](https://in.pinterest.com/pin/caddxfpv-10inch-frame-kit-replacement-for-racing-drone-carbon-fiber-x-7-inches-frame-for-fpv--4592545847708200960/)). Treat "CADDX" as the brand on the listing, with the real factory or OEM unknown. The Chinese CNC shops that cut generic Mark4 10-inch frames, such as Tarot, Xianning Tyi Model and Shenzhen Jinquanjiu, all offer OEM branding ([Alibaba Mark4 V2 10](https://electronics.alibaba.com/product/mark-4-v2-10-inch-quadcopter-frame); [Made-in-China Tyi](https://tyi-model.en.made-in-china.com/product/qxvRtirPgwpX/China-Mark4-10-Inch-Fpv-Drone-Frame-Carbon-Fiber-Uav-Frame-Kit-Drone-RC-Arm-Thickness-7mm-Accessory.html)), but none lists a Knight or an 8 mm option.

Four products carry the name. The **Knight 10" V2 (8mm)** bare frame costs **1250 UAH, down from 1450 UAH**, and BraveTech shows it in stock with delivery in Ukraine within five days. The listing was about 224 days old when indexed, which dates it to roughly early 2026. The listing markets the frame for military use as well as long-range FPV ([BraveTech](https://thebravetech.com/product/frame-caddx-knight-10-v2-8mm/)). BraveTech also sells a smaller **Knight 08 V2 (8mm)** ([BraveTech Knight 08](https://thebravetech.com/product/frame-caddx-knight-08-v2-8mm/)) and Knight 10 spare parts at about 25 UAH each, with the parts not named in the summary ([BraveTech frames](https://thebravetech.com/product-category/fpv/frames/)). The Russian aggregator rcsearch.ru lists a "Caddx Knight 10" frame kit" at two shops, one at about **5359 RUB**, without specs ([rcsearch.ru](https://rcsearch.ru/-c50479)). Russian shops also sell a **ready-built Caddx Knight 10 quad** at about 44,990–55,200 RUB with a **786 g dry weight** without battery, marketed for military use ([copterparts.ru](https://copterparts.ru/product/%D0%BA%D0%B2%D0%B0%D0%B4%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%BF%D1%82%D0%B5%D1%80-fpv-caddx-knight-10-vtx-1-2-elrs-915/); [fnsmart.ru](https://fnsmart.ru/kvadrokopter-fpv-caddx-knight10-1-2-elrs915/)). A Ukrainian built quad, the "POMSTA 10", names "CADDX Knight 10 V2" as its frame ([Defpoint](https://defpoint.ua/en/drony-ta-zapchastyny/fpv-drony/fpv-dron-10-dyuimiv-en)). The "V2" implies an earlier V1, perhaps with thinner arms, but no V1 listing was found. No English-language retailer, AliExpress, Amazon, eBay or Banggood listing exists in the results. The frame's price sits inside the 975–1395 UAH band for generic Mark4 V2 10-inch frames in Ukraine ([Prom.ua](https://prom.ua/ua/Rama-10-dyujmov.html); [fpvkit](https://fpvkit.com.ua/en/products/frame-kit-mark4-10-v2)).

Three things stand out. First, the **8 mm arm is the Knight's only distinctive published figure**. No Chinese factory listing for a Mark4 10-inch states 8 mm, and the arm-thickness options OEMs advertise are 6.0, 7 and 7.5 mm ([rcdrone.top Boscam](https://rcdrone.top/products/boscam-mark-4-v2-10-inch-427mm-carbon-fiber-fpv-frame-kit)). Second, the **35 mm internal height matches the Mark4 V2's 35 mm standoffs** rather than GEPRC's 30 mm. Third, no middle plate is listed, so the Knight may be a two-plate stack rather than the three-plate Mark4 sandwich, though a summary could simply have dropped it. The 415 mm wheelbase is not unique either: BraveTech's own catalogue carries two frames at 415 mm, so identify the frame by name and outline, not wheelbase alone ([BraveTech frames](https://thebravetech.com/product-category/fpv/frames/)).

## Your 8 mm arms fit the Knight, but the hole pattern cannot be checked

Your frame has a rear standoff pair about 19–20 mm apart, a second pair at 20 mm, a much wider front pair, inner arm holes about 24 mm apart and possibly 8 mm arms. Only the arm thickness can be compared with published Knight data, and it matches. Every hole-pattern figure you have is missing from the Knight listing, so a match is **plausible but unproven**.

The reference CAD you already have gives a sharper picture. The GEPRC 7-inch STEP has standoff pairs of 31.4 / 20 / 20 / 19 mm from front to rear, with inner arm holes at 20 and 36 mm. The RJX Mark4 V2 10-inch (427 mm) STEP has 31.4 / 24 / 24 / 19 mm. Your 20 mm second pair and ~19–20 mm rear pair follow the GEPRC sequence, not the V2's 24 mm middle pairs. Your ~24 mm inner arm-hole spacing matches neither file directly, though 24 mm equals the V2's middle standoff-pair width, which may or may not be a coincidence. These are your own measurements of your own files, not published data, and no public source gives standoff or arm-bolt spacings as text for any 10-inch Mark4 or for the Knight.

That leaves three explanations, roughly in order of fit. The first is that **your frame is the Knight 10" V2**: a Mark4-like 415 mm cut with its own standoff layout and 8 mm arms. This fits the arm thickness and the name, and nothing contradicts it. The second is a **Mark4 V2 10-inch with 8 mm arms sold under a shop label**. A Prom.ua listing offers a 427 mm Mark4 V2 with 8 mm arms at 1100 UAH ([Prom.ua](https://prom.ua/p2132751948-rama-dlya-drona.html)), and 8 mm fiberglass replacement arms for the 427 mm V2 are sold on Rozetka ([Rozetka](https://rozetka.com.ua/ua/459333884/p459333884/)). Your GEPRC-style 20 mm pairs sit awkwardly with the V2 body, though. The third is a **different 8 mm 10-inch frame**, such as an iFlight XL10 V6 clone sold with 8 mm arms at 420 mm ([Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/BayDe-10inch-Compatible-System-Replacement/dp/B0CPL9PHMS)). Also remember that a 7.5 mm V2 arm can read close to 8 mm on calipers once weave and coating are included.

## Six measurements will identify the frame

Measure in this order, because the first reading eliminates the most candidates.

| Measure | Points to Knight 10" V2 | Points elsewhere |
|---|---|---|
| **Motor-to-motor diagonal** (centre of one motor's bolt circle to the opposite motor's) | **~415 mm** (also HGLRC MY10) | 427 mm Mark4 V2; 428–429 mm GEPRC; 420 mm XL10 V6; 418 mm iFlight Helion 10 |
| **Adjacent motor spacing** (front-left to front-right, and front-left to rear-left) | ~293 mm on both sides if it is a true X (computed: 415 ÷ √2) | ~302 mm on both sides for a 427–428 mm true X; unequal sides mean a stretched X |
| **Outline** (overall length × width of the arms) | Unknown for the Knight; record it for the seller | ~268 × 332 mm Mark4 V2; ~303–305 × 370–376 mm GEPRC/UDC; 310 × 365 mm HGLRC MY10 |
| **Arm thickness and material** (calipers on a bare cut edge, away from coating) | 8.0 mm carbon twill | ~7.5 mm suggests a V2 arm read high; a matte grey-green laminate is fiberglass |
| **Plate stack** (number of plates, plate thicknesses, standoff length and diameter) | 2 mm top, 3 mm bottom, ~35 mm internal height | Three plates with 30 mm standoffs suggests GEPRC; three plates with 35 mm × Ø6 suggests a V2 |
| **Bare weight** (kitchen scale, no hardware) | Unknown; record it | 238–270 g Mark4 V2 and GEPRC; 217 g HGLRC MY10 |

Sources for the reference figures: the table in the previous section, plus the Helion 10 at 418 mm ([iFlight](https://shop.iflight.com/Helion-10-10inch-Frame-Kit-Pro2093)), the XL10 V6 at 420 mm ([iFlight](https://shop.iflight.com/xl10-v6-10-inch-fpv-frame-Pro1967)) and the UDC Mark4 10 at about 305 × 370 mm ([UDC](https://www.udc.net.ua/en/products/udc-mark4-10)). The adjacent-spacing figures are simple geometry, not published data. Also check the motor holes (16×16, 19×19 or both) and look for engravings or a logo on the plates. Then ask BraveTech for the outline, standoff positions and a photo of the bottom plate next to a ruler. That one photo would allow a hole-for-hole comparison with your 19–20 / 20 / wide-front pattern and 24 mm arm holes, which no public source can provide today.

## No Knight CAD exists; the nearest STEP files are GEPRC's

Searches of GrabCAD, Thingiverse, Printables, Cults3D, MakerWorld, CGTrader and Sketchfab found **no CAD, STEP, DXF or dimensioned drawing** for any "Knight" or "MK4 Knight" 10-inch frame. The only near hit was a camera pan/tilt accessory for a Mark4 10-inch frame ([Cults3D](https://cults3d.com/:1074952)). No video, Reddit, RCGroups, IntoFPV or indexed Telegram post shows or measures the frame. The BraveTech listing presumably carries product photos, but they could not be viewed because page fetches were blocked. Opening that page in a browser is the quickest way to judge the arm outline by eye.

If your frame turns out to be a Mark4 derivative, these 10-inch models are available for comparison. On Cults3D there are "GEPRC MK4 10in" as STEP ([Cults3D](https://cults3d.com/en/3d-model/gadget/geprc-mk4-10in)), "GEPRC MK4 V2 10in" as STL ([Cults3D](https://cults3d.com/en/3d-model/gadget/geprc-mk4-v2-10in-fpv-frame)) and a generic "Frame Mark 4 10 inch" STEP set ([Cults3D](https://cults3d.com/en/3d-model/gadget/frame-mark-4-10-inch-step-cad-files)). GrabCAD has a full-frame "GEPRC Mark 4 Frame (5–10 inch)" model and a "GEPRC MK4 V2 10in" model whose uploader claims more accurate hole placement ([GrabCAD likes listing](https://grabcad.com/andy.lo-15/likes)). None of these is the Knight. At 415 mm, a Knight-shaped model would need to be built from measurements of a real frame.

## Conclusion

The search changes the question. "MK4 Knight" is not a Mark4 variant you can look up. It is almost certainly the CADDX Knight 10" V2, a Ukrainian-market 415 mm frame whose only published distinctive feature, the 8 mm arm, happens to be the one thing you already suspected. The sources cannot confirm or rule out a match on hole pattern, because the data that would decide it (standoff positions, arm-bolt spacing, outline) has never been published. Your own frame is now a better reference than anything online. If the diagonal reads about 415 mm, measure and record the full plate geometry, since that would be the first public dimensional record of this frame. If it reads 427–428 mm, drop the Knight lead and compare against the GEPRC and V2 STEP files instead.

Remember that every number in this report came from search-engine summaries. The 415 mm and 8 mm figures appeared consistently across several summaries of one shop page. That is one seller's claim seen through a search engine, not an independent second source, so verify it on the live BraveTech page before relying on it.
