# MK4 Knight 10-inch frame: CAD files, drawings, videos and community posts

Method note: about 20 web searches in English, Russian, Ukrainian and Chinese, covering CAD sites, forums, video and shops. Every page fetch failed. WebFetch returned DNS errors (ENOTFOUND for thebravetech.com and defpoint.ua), and curl through the proxy got `CONNECT tunnel failed, response 403`. All findings below therefore come from search-engine summaries of the pages. None of them was read in full or checked against an image or drawing.

## Are there CAD or 3D models named Knight / MK4 Knight on GrabCAD, Thingiverse, Printables, Cults3D, MakerWorld, CGTrader, Sketchfab?

### Takeaway
No. I found no CAD file, 3D model or dimensioned drawing named "MK4 Knight", "Mark4 Knight" or "Knight 10" on any of these sites. The only CAD for 10" Mark4 frames is for GEPRC Mark4 / MK4 V2 models on Cults3D and GrabCAD, and none of it is labelled Knight.

- A search for "MK4 Knight" together with STEP/STL/GrabCAD/Thingiverse/Printables returned only unrelated items: a Prusa MK4 printer space model, a Cults3D "Generic MK4 Weapons" miniatures collection, and unrelated GrabCAD user pages. — [Printables Prusa MK4 model](https://www.printables.com/model/961911-prusa-mk4-model); [Cults3D collection](https://cults3d.com/en/creations/generic-mk4-weapons/design-collections)

The closest hit was a Cults3D accessory, a "Pan/Tilt Gear Mechanism for Caddx Ratel Pro – Mark 4 (10") Frame", which is a camera mount and not frame geometry. MakerWorld had only an unrelated "Knight" robot figure. — [Cults3D (accessory)](https://cults3d.com/:1074952); [MakerWorld](https://www.makerworld.com/en/models/2176596)
- 10" Mark4 CAD that does exist is all GEPRC-based:
  - Cults3D lists "GEPRC MK4 10in" as a STEP file and "GEPRC MK4 V2 10in FPV frame" as an STL. — [Cults3D GEPRC MK4 10in](https://cults3d.com/en/3d-model/gadget/geprc-mk4-10in); [Cults3D GEPRC MK4 V2 10in](https://cults3d.com/en/3d-model/gadget/geprc-mk4-v2-10in-fpv-frame)

I saw both only through a GrabCAD user's "likes" page in search results. — [GrabCAD likes listing](https://grabcad.com/andy.lo-15/likes)
- "BETAFPV X-Knight" is an unrelated small toothpick frame line (3", 4", 5" and a 360 version, 4 mm arms, 26.5 mm FC mount). — [BETAFPV X-Knight](https://betafpv.com/products/x-knight-4inch-fpv-racing-carbon-fiber-frame-kit)

### Inferences
- The product the user knows as "MK4 Knight" / "Knight 10" is most likely the **CADDX Knight 10"** frame (see the next section). No public CAD of it was found under any name.
- The GEPRC MK4 10" STEP files on Cults3D and GrabCAD are the nearest downloadable CAD. They are a different frame from the Knight.

### Gaps
- I could not search inside Sketchfab, CGTrader, Thingiverse or Printables directly (the pages could not be fetched), so a model that the search engines have not indexed may exist.
- No DXF or cutting files for the Knight frame were found. Clone-frame makers rarely publish them.

### Inferences
- "CADDX Knight 10"" looks like a frame and drone sold in the Ukrainian and Russian markets, not a product listed on CADDX's own global site. I found no manufacturer page. The "V2 (8mm)" name suggests an earlier version existed, possibly with thinner arms. That is unconfirmed.
- Its 415 mm wheelbase is shorter than the Mark4 V2 10" (427 mm) and the GEPRC Mark4-10 (428 mm), and matches several other 10" frames such as the HGLRC MY10 (415 mm, 7 mm arms). If the Knight is a Mark4 derivative, it is a resized one rather than a straight clone of the 427/428 mm geometry.

## What do the available dimensions say about whether it matches the user's hole pattern?

### Takeaway
The available data neither confirms nor rules out a match. The **8 mm arm thickness** matches the user's "possibly 8 mm arms" and differs from GEPRC Mark4-10 (7 mm) and Mark4 V2 427 mm (7.5 mm) frames. The 20×20 / 30.5×30.5 stacks are generic. No source gives standoff positions, standoff pair spacings, arm-bolt spacing or the outline.

### Cited Findings
- Knight 10" V2: 415 mm wheelbase, 8 mm arms, 2 mm top plate, 3 mm bottom plate, 20×20 / 30.5×30.5 mm stacks, about 35 mm internal height, 16–19 mm motor mounts. — [BraveTech](https://thebravetech.com/product/frame-caddx-knight-10-v2-8mm/)
- Reference frames for comparison:
  - GEPRC Mark4 10": 428 mm motor-to-motor, 7 mm arms, 3 mm base plate. — [GetFPV GEPRC Mark4 10"](https://www.getfpv.com/geprc-mark4-10-frame-kit.html)
  - Mark4 V2 10" 427 mm: 7.5 mm arms per several listings. One Amazon listing (Hylukon MAK4 V2) says 6 mm arms with a 3 mm base and 2 mm top plate, so arm thickness varies between clone sellers. — [CxzHtv Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Mark4-10inch-Carbon-Quadcopter-Freestyle/dp/B0FJFLSK7H); [Hylukon Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Hylukon-Control-Freestyle-Distance-Quadcopter/dp/B0D5M61LV1); [Readytosky Mark4 10" V2 (Rotorama)](https://www.rotorama.com/product/readytosky-mark4-10-v2)
- The Knight V2's 2 mm top and 3 mm bottom plates match the common Mark4 V2 427 mm plate thicknesses (3 mm bottom/middle, 2 mm top). — [FlyTeam Mark4 10 V2](https://flyteam.com.ua/frame/mark410v2); [Hylukon Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Hylukon-Control-Freestyle-Distance-Quadcopter/dp/B0D5M61LV1)
- Other 10" frames with 8 mm arms exist, for example an XL10 V6 420 mm variant sold with 8 mm arms. So 8 mm arms alone do not identify the Knight. — [Amazon XL10 V6 8mm](https://www.amazon.com/BayDe-10inch-Compatible-System-Replacement/dp/B0CPL9PHMS)

### Inferences
- The user's frame has 8 mm arms and an outline that differs from the Mark4 standard: a ~19–20 mm rear pair, a 20 mm second pair, a much wider front pair, and inner arm holes ~24 mm apart. That is consistent with a Mark4-derived frame such as the Knight 10" V2 at 415 mm, but only arm and plate thickness can be checked against published data.
- The user's ~24 mm inner arm-hole spacing differs from the GEPRC 7" STEP (20/36 mm). It may be closer to RJX Mark4 V2 10" practice, where the middle standoff pairs are 24 mm apart. That is a weak, unverified link.
- Wheelbase gives a practical test. Measure the user's frame diagonally from motor centre to motor centre. About 415 mm points to the Knight 10" V2 (or a similar 415 mm frame such as the HGLRC MY10). About 427–428 mm points to a Mark4 V2 or GEPRC-family frame.

### Gaps
- Knight 10" dimensions not found in any source: standoff positions and pair spacings, arm-bolt (inner and outer arm hole) spacing, plate outline, arm length, weight, and the camera-plate width or front standoff spacing.
- No drawing, STEP, DXF or measured photo of the Knight frame was found, so a hole-for-hole comparison with the user's frame is not possible from public sources. Options are a direct measurement, or asking BraveTech or another seller for a drawing or a photo with a ruler.
