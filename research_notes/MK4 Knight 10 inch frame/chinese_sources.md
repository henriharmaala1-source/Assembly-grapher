# MK4 Knight 10-inch FPV frame: Chinese and Asian manufacturer sources

Method note: about 13 web searches in English, Simplified Chinese, Russian and Ukrainian, covering "MK4 Knight", "Mark4 Knight", "骑士 MK4 10寸 机架", "MK4骑士 / 骑士MK4 / Mark4骑士", "骑士 穿越机 机架 10寸", "Knight 10 FPV frame", "MK4 10寸 机架 8mm 机臂 1688", "Knight 10 aliexpress mark4", and Alibaba / Made-in-China OEM queries. Every page fetch failed at DNS (`getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND`) through the proxy: tarotrc.com, aliexpress.com and tyi-model.en.made-in-china.com. So the figures below come from search-engine summaries and snippets, not from pages I read myself. Treat each number as "stated by the listing per search summary" and check it on the live page. Taobao, 1688 product pages and 5iMX threads did not show up as specific results for any "Knight" query.

## Q1. Is there a Chinese or Asian factory or store selling an "MK4 Knight" / "Mark4 Knight" / "骑士 MK4" 10-inch frame, possibly the OEM behind resold versions?

### Takeaway
No Chinese or Asian source turned up that sells a 10-inch frame named "MK4 Knight", "Mark4 Knight" or "骑士" in any language I searched. The name looks like a reseller or regional trade name, not a factory model name. The nearest factory-direct products are generic "Mark4 V2 10寸 427mm" clones. These come from several Chinese CNC shops (Tarot / 温州飞越, Xianning Tyi Model, Shenzhen Jinquanjiu, Readytosky, RJXHobby, Boscam, and others) plus the original GEPRC Mark4 10. Any of them could be the OEM behind a "Knight"-branded resale, but I found nothing that ties a specific factory to the name.

### Inferences
- "MK4 Knight" is probably a name given by a reseller or regional distributor (possibly Ukrainian or European), not a Chinese factory SKU. That would explain why no Chinese-language listing uses 骑士 for a 10" frame. It could also be a shop's own name for a private-label Mark4 V2 cut by one of the CNC shops above.
- The most common OEM base for 10" "Mark4" resales is the 427 mm Mark4 V2 pattern. The suspected 8 mm arm points away from the stock 7.5 mm Mark4 V2 and toward a reinforced variant or a custom cut.

### Gaps
- No primary listing for "MK4 Knight" was found anywhere. To pin down the OEM, I would need the user's own seller link, the packaging or plate engravings, or a photo.
- Taobao, 1688 product pages, Global Sources and 5iMX did not return specific results for any Knight or 骑士 query, and their pages could not be fetched.

## Q2. What arm thickness (8 mm suspected), wheelbase and plate thicknesses do Chinese sources state for MK4 10" frames?

### Takeaway
No Chinese MK4 10" listing in the results states an 8 mm arm. Chinese sources state 7–7.5 mm arms, a 427–429 mm wheelbase (some listings 450–473 mm), 2–3 mm top plates and 2.5–3 mm bottom plates. The 10" kits weigh about 227–270 g.

### Cited Findings

  - 7.5 mm arms, 3 mm bottom plate, 3 mm top plate
  - FC/VTX holes 30.5 mm and 20 mm
  - Price ¥205 RMB (data about three years old)

  — [Tarot TL1613](http://www.tarotrc.com/Product/Detail.aspx?Lang=zh&Id=56801373-f309-4c85-b175-436961af57b2)

### Inferences
- If the user's "Knight" frame really has 8 mm arms, it is not stock Mark4 V2 (7.5 mm) or GEPRC (7 mm). A caliper reading of about 7.5 mm on a stock Mark4 V2 arm could be rounded up to "8 mm".

### Gaps
- None of the snippets gave standoff count, height or diameter, the VTX hole pattern, or the motor-mount pattern for these Chinese 10" listings. The usual Mark4 V2 10" motor mount (16×16/19×19 M3 is common for 2810–3115 class motors) could not be confirmed because the pages were blocked.
- No 8 mm arm statement was found in any Chinese source.

## Q3. Does any factory offer custom arm thicknesses (for example 7 or 8 mm) or OEM cutting?

### Takeaway
Yes, in general. Chinese Mark4 10" suppliers advertise OEM branding and a choice of arm thickness, but the stated options are 6.0, 7 and 7.5 mm. None of the sources found explicitly offers an 8 mm option.

### Cited Findings
- The Boscam Mark4 V2 10" is described as having a 427 mm wheelbase with a "customizable arm thickness of 7.5 mm or 6.0 mm". — [rcdrone.top Boscam Mark4 V2](https://rcdrone.top/products/boscam-mark-4-v2-10-inch-427mm-carbon-fiber-fpv-frame-kit)
- Shenzhen Jinquanjiu sells the Mark4 V2 10" as OEM-branded on Alibaba. — [Alibaba Mark4 V2 10 guide](https://electronics.alibaba.com/product/mark-4-v2-10-inch-quadcopter-frame)
- Xianning Tyi Model offers a 7 mm arm version on Made-in-China at a stated capacity of 5000 pcs/year. — [Made-in-China Tyi](https://tyi-model.en.made-in-china.com/product/qxvRtirPgwpX/China-Mark4-10-Inch-Fpv-Drone-Frame-Carbon-Fiber-Uav-Frame-Kit-Drone-RC-Arm-Thickness-7mm-Accessory.html)
- 1688 listings describe MK4 10寸 carbon frames as "CNC加工配件批发" (CNC-machined parts, wholesale). This implies job-shop cutting. — [1688 picture index](https://tw.1688.com/pic/-BBFABCDCBCFECDBCC6AC.html?beginPage=2)

### Inferences
- Chinese carbon CNC shops commonly cut 3K plate in 1 mm steps (often up to 8–10 mm). An 8 mm arm is therefore probably available on request from these OEMs, but this is inference and no source states it.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm explicit 8 mm options, MOQ for custom cuts, or custom-cut pricing, because supplier pages were not fetchable.
