## Which Ukrainian or Eastern European shops list an "MK4 Knight" 10" frame?

### Takeaway
No search found a product called "MK4 Knight", "Mark4 Knight", "Лицар" or "Рыцарь" as a 10-inch frame in Ukrainian, Russian or Polish sources. The only "Knight" 10-inch frame in Eastern European shops is the **CADDX Knight 10" V2 (8mm)**. The Ukrainian shop BraveTech sells it, and the Russian aggregator rcsearch.ru lists it. Russian shops also sell a ready-built Caddx Knight 10 drone. "MK4 Knight" is most likely a mix-up of "Mark4" and "Caddx Knight", or a seller's informal name. I found no evidence for that, though.

## What arm thickness and material are stated?

### Takeaway
The CADDX Knight 10" V2 is listed with **8 mm carbon-fibre (вуглепластик) arms**. No fibreglass/склотекстоліт version was found. Plain Mark4 10" V2 clones sold in Ukraine use 7.5 mm carbon arms.

### Cited Findings
- CADDX Knight 10" V2 (8mm) specs, as given in search summaries of the listing:
  - frame type X-Type, wheelbase 415 mm, material carbon fibre (вуглепластик)
  - top plate 2 mm, bottom plate 3 mm, arms 8 mm
  - stack mounting 20x20 mm and 30.5x30.5 mm, motor mounting Ф16–19 mm
  - internal height 35 mm, aimed at long-range builds

  [BraveTech](https://thebravetech.com/product/frame-caddx-knight-10-v2-8mm/)
- One search summary said the BraveTech listing was about 224 days old at search time — [BraveTech](https://thebravetech.com/product/frame-caddx-knight-10-v2-8mm/)
- For comparison, the Mark4 10" V2 sold by fpvkit.com.ua is listed as X-type, 427 mm wheelbase, 268.18 x 332.07 mm, 3 mm bottom and middle plates, 2 mm top plate and 7.5 mm arms — [fpvkit Mark4 10" v2](https://fpvkit.com.ua/en/products/frame-kit-mark4-10-v2)
- Other Mark4 V2 10" listings also give 7.5 mm arms and a 427 mm wheelbase — [AliExpress Mark4 V2](https://www.aliexpress.com/item/1005005897270664.html); [Rotorama Readytosky Mark4 10 V2](https://www.rotorama.com/product/readytosky-mark4-10-v2)
- Caddx's different X10 frame is listed with 6 mm arms and 338 x 299 x 34 mm. It is not the Knight — [hobbypark.by Caddx X10](https://hobbypark.by/catalog/kvadrokoptery_professionalnye/kvadrokopter_caddx_x10_komplektatsiya_ir/)

### Inferences
- If the user's frame is the Caddx Knight 10 V2, the suspected 8 mm arm matches the listing.
- No middle-plate thickness was given for the Knight. Its 2 mm top and 3 mm bottom plates fit a two-plate stack with a 35 mm internal height, rather than the three-plate Mark4 V2 (3/3/2 mm). This is inferred from the spec list only.

## What wheelbase and outline are stated, and is it a GEPRC-style or V2-style Mark4 derivative?

### Takeaway
The Knight 10" V2 is listed at **415 mm** wheelbase. That fits neither the GEPRC Mark4 10 (428 mm, 303 x 376 mm) nor the Mark4 V2 10 (427 mm, 268 x 332 mm). I found no outline dimensions for it.

### Cited Findings
- Knight 10" V2 wheelbase is 415 mm. BraveTech's catalog has two frames listed at 415 mm, so identify the frame by model name, not wheelbase — [BraveTech Knight 10 V2](https://thebravetech.com/product/frame-caddx-knight-10-v2-8mm/); [BraveTech frames category](https://thebravetech.com/product-category/fpv/frames/)
- GEPRC Mark4 10" reference: 376 x 303 mm, 428 mm motor-to-motor (one listing says 429 mm), 270 g — [RMRC GEPRC Mark4 10](https://www.readymaderc.com/products/details/86588-geprc-mark4-10-multirotor-frame); [TBS GEPRC Mark4 10](https://www.team-blacksheep.com/products/product:7652)
- Mark4 V2 10" reference: 427 mm, 268.18 x 332.07 mm, 7.5 mm arms — [fpvkit Mark4 10" v2](https://fpvkit.com.ua/en/products/frame-kit-mark4-10-v2); [UDC Mark4 10](https://www.udc.net.ua/en/products/udc-mark4-10)
- Readytosky Mark4 10" V2 listing gives conflicting diagonals (473 mm and 427 mm) — [Rotorama](https://www.rotorama.com/product/readytosky-mark4-10-v2)
- Other 10" frames near 415 mm in the region: HGLRC MY10 at 415 mm with 7 mm arms, iFlight Nazgul XL10 V6 at 420 mm with 7 mm arms (8 mm in some clone listings), Pilotix PT10 V2 at 425 mm with 7 mm arms — [fpv24 HGLRC MY10](https://www.fpv24.com/en/hglrc/hglrc-my10-10-inch-heavy-lift-long-range-fpv-frame); [openelab Nazgul XL10 V6 (PL)](https://openelab.io/pl/products/iflight-nazgul-xl10-v6-10); [Amazon XL10 V6 8 mm clone](https://www.amazon.com/BayDe-10inch-Compatible-System-Replacement/dp/B0CPL9PHMS); [Rotorama Pilotix PT10 V2](https://www.rotorama.com/product/pilotix-frame-pilotix-pt10-v2)

### Inferences
- A 415 mm wheelbase points to a separate design, or a shortened X derivative, not a direct copy of either Mark4 variant. Its 415 mm and 8 mm arms are closer to long-range frames like the HGLRC MY10 (415 mm) and the XL10 V6 8 mm clones (420 mm). Whether it is a Mark4 derivative at all is unconfirmed.
- If the user's frame measures about 427–428 mm motor-to-motor, it is probably a Mark4 V2 or GEPRC clone sold under a "Knight" name, not the Caddx Knight 10 V2. A measured motor-to-motor diagonal would settle this.

### Gaps
- I found no outline (length x width) or height-over-plates for the Knight 10 V2.
- I found no published standoff or arm-bolt spacing for any "Knight" 10" frame.
