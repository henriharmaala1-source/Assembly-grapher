# MK4 Knight 10-inch FPV frame: English-language sources

Method note: about 18 web searches with spelling variants ("MK4 Knight", "Mark4 Knight", "MK4-Knight", "Mark 4 Knight", "Knight 10", "Knight 10 V2", "Caddx Knight", plus Ukrainian/Russian "рама Knight 10"). Every page fetch I tried was blocked: WebFetch returned DNS errors (thebravetech.com, defpoint.ua) and curl got a 403 from the proxy on CONNECT (thebravetech.com, rcsearch.ru; prom.ua was also denied in the proxy log). All findings below therefore come from **search-engine summaries and snippets, not from pages I read in full**. Treat every number as unverified until someone checks the listing page.

## Q1. Who makes or sells a frame called "MK4 Knight" / "Mark4 Knight" (10")?

### Takeaway
No English-language shop, marketplace or brand site I could find lists a product called "MK4 Knight", "Mark4 Knight" or "MK4-Knight". The only real 10" "Knight" frame that turned up is the **"CADDX Knight 10" V2 (8mm)"**, sold by the Ukrainian retailer BraveTech and listed in a Russian price aggregator. It is probably the frame the user means. The "MK4" part of the name may be the seller's or the user's shorthand, since this frame follows the Mark4 family layout.

### Cited Findings
- Exact searches for "MK4 Knight", "Mark4 Knight", "MK4-Knight", "Mark 4 Knight" and "MK4 Knight 10" found no matching FPV product. They found only generic Mark4 / MAK4 V2 frames (Amazon, Walmart, Captain RC, Rotorama, GEPRC) and unrelated "Mark IV" / Knight's Armament items. — [Amazon Hylukon MAK4 V2 10in 427mm](https://www.amazon.com/Hylukon-Control-Freestyle-Distance-Quadcopter/dp/B0D5M61LV1); [Captain RC MARK4 V2 8/9/10"](https://captainrc.com/products/3k-carbon-fiber-frame-mark4-v2-8inch-367mm-9inch-387mm-10inch-427mm-wheelbase-rc-fpv-freestyle-racing-drone-frame-kit); [Rotorama Readytosky Mark4 10" V2](https://www.rotorama.com/product/readytosky-mark4-10-v2); [GEPRC GEP-Mark4](https://geprc.com/product/gep-mark4-frame/)
- **Main candidate:** a Ukrainian-language product page titled "Рама CADDX Knight 10" V2 (8mm)" ("CADDX Knight 10" V2 (8mm) frame") at BraveTech. — [BraveTech: CADDX Knight 10" V2 (8mm)](https://thebravetech.com/product/frame-caddx-knight-10-v2-8mm/)
- A Russian price-comparison page lists "Caddx Knight 10" frame kit" at two shops but gives no specs. The "V2" naming implies an earlier V1 exists, but I found no V1 listing. — [rcsearch.ru: Caddx Knight 10" frame kit](https://rcsearch.ru/-c50479)
- One search summary said a Ukrainian retailer's ready-to-fly 10" FPV build (the "POMSTA 10 inches" listing at defpoint.ua) names "CADDX Knight 10 V2 10'" as its frame. I could not open the page to confirm. — [defpoint.ua: FPV drone POMSTA 10 inches](https://defpoint.ua/en/drony-ta-zapchastyny/fpv-drony/fpv-dron-10-dyuimiv-en)

Caddx is known for FPV cameras and digital video systems, and the results showed no frame catalogue from Caddx. — [search results were BETAFPV X-Knight pages, e.g. Unmanned Tech: BETAFPV X-Knight 35](https://www.unmannedtechshop.co.uk/products/betafpv-x-knight-35-fpv-drone-frame)

### Inferences
- "MK4 Knight" is most likely the CADDX Knight 10" V2, a frame sold mainly through Ukrainian and Russian retail channels. Mark4 is the generic family name for this kind of frame in those markets, so "MK4" was probably added either by a seller or in conversation.
- The "CADDX" label is not confirmed as a real Caddx (Walksnail parent) product. It could be a reseller label or a factory or OEM name. Treat the maker as unknown, with "CADDX" as the brand named on the listing.
- Candidates checked and rejected: BETAFPV X-Knight (wrong size). "Night", "Nightcrawler", "Knight Rider" and "KNGHT" variants produced no FPV frame hits in the searches I ran.

### Gaps
- I could not open any listing page, so there are no confirmed photos, image URLs, manual or package contents.
- The actual manufacturer or OEM behind "CADDX Knight" is unknown.
- I found no English-language retailer (GetFPV, RDQ, Pyrodrone, Banggood, AliExpress, Amazon, eBay) that lists it.

## Q2. Frame specifications (arm thickness, wheelbase, plates, standoffs, hole patterns, weight)

### Takeaway
According to the BraveTech listing as summarized by the search engine, the CADDX Knight 10" V2 is a carbon-fiber true-X 10" frame. It has **8 mm arms**, which matches what the user suspected, a **415 mm wheelbase**, a 2 mm top plate, a 3 mm bottom plate, about 35 mm of internal height, 20×20 and 30.5×30.5 mm stack mounts, and motor mounts for 16–19 mm hole patterns. Weight, outline dimensions and standoff details were not available.

### Cited Findings
- Material: carbon fiber (Ukrainian "вуглепластик"), X-type layout, described as long-range geometry. — [BraveTech](https://thebravetech.com/product/frame-caddx-knight-10-v2-8mm/) (search summary)
- Wheelbase: **415 mm**. — [BraveTech](https://thebravetech.com/product/frame-caddx-knight-10-v2-8mm/) (search summary, given the same way in three separate searches)
- Arm thickness: **8 mm** (in the product title "(8mm)" and in the spec list). — [BraveTech](https://thebravetech.com/product/frame-caddx-knight-10-v2-8mm/)
- Plates: top **2 mm**, bottom **3 mm**. — [BraveTech](https://thebravetech.com/product/frame-caddx-knight-10-v2-8mm/) (search summary)
- Internal height between plates: about **35 mm**. This implies standoffs of about 35 mm; their count and diameter were not stated. — [BraveTech](https://thebravetech.com/product/frame-caddx-knight-10-v2-8mm/) (search summary)
- FC/stack mounting: **20×20 mm and 30.5×30.5 mm**. — [BraveTech](https://thebravetech.com/product/frame-caddx-knight-10-v2-8mm/)
- Motor mounts: **16–19 mm** hole patterns (16×16 / 19×19, M3). — [BraveTech](https://thebravetech.com/product/frame-caddx-knight-10-v2-8mm/)
- The VTX mount pattern, camera mount width, weight, outline dimensions, standoff count and diameter, and arm-bolt spacing did not appear in any snippet.

### Inferences
- Since an 8 mm arm and a 3 mm bottom plate are given separately, the arms are probably separate plates clamped between the bottom plate and a lower or arm plate, as on Mark4 V2-style 10" frames. That layout is not confirmed.
- 2 + 3 + 8 mm plates with about 35 mm internal height are in line with other heavy-duty 10" frames. For comparison, the HGLRC MY10 has a 415 mm wheelbase, **7 mm** arms, a 310 × 365 mm outline and weighs 217 g ±5 g (frame only). — [GetFPV HGLRC MY10 10" LR](https://www.getfpv.com/multi-rotor-frames/6-quad-frames/hglrc-my10-10-lr-frame-kit.html) (search summary). Use these numbers only as a size reference, not as Knight specs.

### Gaps
- Weight, outline dimensions, standoff count, diameter (M3 or M4) and exact length, arm-bolt spacing, VTX and camera mount patterns, arm width and hardware list were not found.
- The specs could not be checked against a second independent seller.

## Q3. Is it a Mark4 clone (GEPRC 428 mm / V2 427 mm) or its own design?

### Takeaway
It is not a straight Mark4 V2 clone. The stated 415 mm wheelbase differs from the 427 mm of the generic Mark4 V2 10" (and from the 424–429 mm figures some sellers list). The 8 mm arms and 2 or 3 mm plates are also heavier than a typical V2 10". It looks like its own design, or a different heavy-duty "long-range 10"" template, that resembles the Mark4 family in general.

### Cited Findings
- Generic Mark4 / MAK4 V2 10" frames are listed at a **427 mm** wheelbase (Captain RC, Amazon Hylukon, Walmart, Probots), with **6–7 mm** arms on most English listings. — [Captain RC](https://captainrc.com/products/3k-carbon-fiber-frame-mark4-v2-8inch-367mm-9inch-387mm-10inch-427mm-wheelbase-rc-fpv-freestyle-racing-drone-frame-kit); [Amazon Hylukon](https://www.amazon.com/Hylukon-Control-Freestyle-Distance-Quadcopter/dp/B0D5M61LV1); [Walmart 10X MARK4 V2 427mm](https://www.walmart.com/ip/16652022016); [Probots (6 mm arms)](https://probots.co.in/mak4-v2-10-inch-carbon-fiber-fpv-drone-frame.html)
- Zbotic lists a Mark4 V2 10" with a **424 mm** wheelbase, **7 mm** 3K T300 arms, a weight of 235 g, a 30.5/20 mm FC mount and 16/19 mm motor holes. — [Zbotic](https://zbotic.in/product/mark4-v2-10-inch-carbon-fiber-fpv-frame-424mm-wheelbase/) (search summary)
- Ukrainian sellers also offer Mark4 V2 10" frames with thicker arms. Prom.ua has one with **8 mm** arms at 1100 ₴, and RUSOREZ.CLUB has one with **7.5 mm** arms on a 427 mm base at 1390 ₴. — [Prom.ua Mark4 V2 10"](https://prom.ua/p2132751948-rama-dlya-drona.html); [RUSOREZ.CLUB Mark4 10"](https://rusorez.club/rama-fpv-10-diuimiv-mark4/) (search summaries)

### Inferences
- If the user's "MK4 Knight" really has a 427 or 428 mm wheelbase, it may be one of the 8 mm-arm Mark4 V2 10" variants sold in Ukraine under a store label, not the CADDX Knight (415 mm). The deciding test is the wheelbase measured motor-to-motor diagonally: 415 mm points to the Knight, 427 mm to a Mark4 V2 variant.

### Gaps
- I found no drawing that compares the Knight's arm shape or plate outline with Mark4 V2 geometry.
