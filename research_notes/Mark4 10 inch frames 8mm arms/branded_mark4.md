# Branded Mark4 (MK4) family 10-inch frames: arm thickness, and whether any use 8 mm arms

Research method note: direct page fetches (WebFetch and curl) were refused by this session's egress proxy (`connect_rejected` / DNS failure for getfpv.com, rotorama.com, readymaderc.com, unmannedtechshop.co.uk). Every figure below therefore comes from search-engine result summaries of the cited pages, not from reading the full pages myself. Quotes marked "quoted" are the wording the search summaries attributed to the page. Spot-check the 8 mm listings in a browser before relying on them. As of 2026-10-27.

## Which makers sell a Mark4 / MK4-layout 10" frame, and what do they list?

### Takeaway
No branded true Mark4-layout 10" frame was found that lists 8 mm arms on the maker's own spec sheet or on a mainstream reseller page. GEPRC GEP-Mark4-10, GEPRC Mark4 LR10, Tarot TL1613 and Aocoda list 7 mm. The generic or Readytosky/RJX "Mark4 V2 10" (427 mm) lists 7.5 mm. The only 10" "8 mm arm" listings found are reseller or marketplace listings of the **iFlight XL10 V6**, which is not a Mark4, and iFlight's own shop says 7 mm. The Rekon10 PRO (not a Mark4) also lists 8 mm arms.

### Inferences
- The "3.7 mm" GEPRC figure is almost certainly a copy error repeated across resellers, since the same spec lists give a 7 mm arm plate plus a 3 mm vertical arm bar.
- The 7.5 mm figure belongs to the Readytosky/RJX/generic Mark4 V2 10" cut (427 mm), which looks like one factory design sold under many labels.
- The 473 mm figure that appears on Rotorama's Readytosky page and Anubis RC is probably a different (stretched) cut or a typo. It conflicts with the 427 mm figure on the same Rotorama page.

## Does any version list 8 mm arms in the 10" size? Maker or reseller?

### Takeaway
Within the true Mark4 family, no. The 8 mm listings in the 10" class are for the iFlight XL10 V6 and the Rekon10 PRO, neither of which is a Mark4. For the XL10 V6, the 8 mm figure comes **only from resellers and marketplaces**, and iFlight's own shop lists 7 mm. For the Rekon10 PRO, 8 mm comes from resellers (GetFPV, Rising Sun); the maker's page was not checked.

### Cited Findings
- iFlight official XL10 V6: "Wheelbase: 420mm, Frame Dimension: L297*W297*H35mm, Arm Thickness: 7mm", plates 3 mm, 343±5 g; max stack height 19 mm, max VTX 29 mm; XL10 V5 and V6 parts are not compatible — [iFlight shop](https://shop.iflight.com/xl10-v6-10-inch-fpv-frame-Pro1967); [iFlight XL10 V6 parts](https://shop.iflight.com/drone-frame-cat346/XL10-V6-Replacement-Parts-Pro2044)

### Inferences
- The XL10 V6 "8 mm" figure is most likely a reseller error that spread across marketplaces, or possibly an unannounced revision. The maker says 7 mm, and RMRC's and rcdrone.top's own sibling listings say 7 mm.
- If the parent project needs a true 8 mm-arm 10" Mark4, it probably does not exist as a catalogue item. The closest options are a 7.5 mm Mark4 V2 10" or a custom 8 mm arm cut.

### Gaps

- Rekon FPV's own spec page was not checked.
- iFlight XL10 V5 arm thickness was not found.
- No 2024-2026 "reinforced" or "heavy" Mark4 10" edition with 8 mm arms turned up in search. The only "reinforced" 10" found was the LX10 V2 (a folding frame with 7 mm arms) — [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Reinforced-Foldable-Carbon-Frame-Wheelbase/dp/B0GPNJPSWB)

## Wheelbase and standoff spacing of each

### Takeaway
Wheelbases: GEPRC Mark4 10 is 428/429 mm, the Mark4 V2 10" is 427 mm (with outliers of 424, 425, 450 and 473 mm), Aocoda is 420 mm, the iFlight XL10 V6 is 420 mm and the Rekon10 PRO is 455 mm. Standoff heights come from sellers only: 30 mm for GEPRC, and 35 mm internal height for the generic V2 and DarwinFPV X10. No source gave standoff hole spacing or a drawing.

### Cited Findings
- GEPRC: 428 mm — [GetFPV](https://www.getfpv.com/geprc-mark4-10-frame-kit.html); 429 mm — [rcdrone.top](https://rcdrone.top/products/geprc-gep-mark4-10-429mm-wheelbase-10-inch-fpv-drone-frame); 30 mm standoffs — [UAV Garage](https://uavgarage.com/shop/geprc-gep-mark4-10-frame-kit-for-fpv-drone/)
- Mark4 V2 10": 427 mm with 35 mm internal height — [fpvdroneframe.com](https://fpvdroneframe.com/product/mark4-v2-10-inch-long-range-fpv-frame/); 427 mm and 473 mm both appear on the same page — [Rotorama](https://www.rotorama.com/product/readytosky-mark4-10-v2)
- iFlight XL10 V6: 420 mm, height 35 mm — [iFlight shop](https://shop.iflight.com/xl10-v6-10-inch-fpv-frame-Pro1967)

### Inferences
- A 427 mm Mark4 V2 10" has 7.5 mm arms and a stack area that carries over from the smaller Mark4 V2 sizes. That makes it the closest catalogue part to an "8 mm Mark4 10"" spec.

### Gaps
- No source gave standoff hole spacing (the X/Y between the Ø6 standoffs) or the arm-clamp bolt pattern for any frame. That needs the makers' DXF/STEP files or manuals, which could not be fetched in this session.
