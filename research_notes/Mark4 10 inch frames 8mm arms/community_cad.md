## 1. Do forums, reviews or hobby communities mention a Mark4 10" with 8 mm arms?

### Inferences
- Of the shop listings that give a thickness, the 427 mm "V2" family most often lists 7.5 mm, with 7 mm and 6 mm next. 8 mm is not a mainstream factory option for any Mark4 10" found.
- An 8 mm arm on the user's frame is most likely one of these:
  - a misread 7.5 mm arm (7.5 mm can measure about 7.7–8 mm with the weave and coating);
  - an aftermarket or locally cut arm, such as the 8 mm fiberglass arms on Rozetka;
  - a small-shop variant like the EcoGalactics listing.
- If the arm is a matte or grey-green laminate rather than twill carbon, fiberglass (склотекстоліт, FR4/G10) is plausible. Fiberglass arms are often cut thicker than carbon to make up for lower stiffness.
- The UDC Mark4 10 outline (305 × 370 mm) is close to the GEPRC Mark4 10 outline (303 × 376 mm). That suggests UDC follows the original GEPRC geometry rather than the 427 mm "V2" outline (268 × 332 mm). This is an inference from outline size only.

### Gaps
- The EcoGalactics and Rozetka pages could not be opened, so their full specs could not be read: arm-bolt spacing, whether the arm is one-piece or a pair of plates, and the photos.
- No forum, Discord or YouTube source discussing 8 mm Mark4 10" arms was found in any language searched.

## 3. Which Mark4 versions have 20 mm-wide middle standoff pairs (like GEPRC) vs 24 mm (like RJX)? Which have 24 mm arm-bolt spacing?

### Takeaway
No source gives standoff-pair or arm-bolt spacings in text. The only measured comparisons are the two STEP files the user already has: GEPRC 7" 31.4 / 20 / 20 / 19 mm with inner arm holes 20 and 36 mm, and RJX V2 10" 31.4 / 24 / 24 / 19 mm. Outline dimensions do let frames be sorted into the GEPRC-geometry family or the 427 mm V2 family, which is a first step.

### Cited Findings
- GEPRC-geometry 10" outline is about 376 × 303 mm with 428 mm motor to motor — [RMRC](https://www.readymaderc.com/products/details/86588-geprc-mark4-10-multirotor-frame). UDC Mark4 10 is about 305 × 370 mm — [udc.net.ua](https://www.udc.net.ua/en/products/udc-mark4-10).
- V2-family 10" outline is about 268 × 332 mm with 427 mm wheelbase. This is shared by RJX — [rjxhobby.com](https://www.rjxhobby.com/rjx-mark4-v2-10inch-427mm-carbon-fiber-twill-matte-frame-kit-for-rc-fpv-racing-drone) — and Tarot at 331 × 269 mm — [tarotrc.com](http://www.tarotrc.com/Product/Detail.aspx?Lang=zh&Id=56801373-f309-4c85-b175-436961af57b2).
- Some listings sell the "473 mm" Mark4 10 as a distinct frame with 5 mm arms — [detali.org.ua](https://detali.org.ua/ru/272588-karbonovaya-rama-mark4-dlya-fpv-drona-10-dyuymov-473-mm.html). Other "473 mm" labels appear on 427 mm V2 listings and look like labelling errors; Anubis RC's 473 mm listing says 427 mm in its key features — [Anubis RC](https://anubisrc.com/product/mark4-10inch-v2-frame-kit-t700-toray-cf/); [dronehub.in.ua](https://dronehub.in.ua/product/mark4-v2-10inch/).
- GEPRC sells replacement arms for the original Mark4 in 7", 8" and 10" — [Rotorama](https://www.rotorama.com/product/geprc-mark4-dily). Some shops list GEPRC Mark4 spares only in 5", 6" and 7" — [Unmanned Tech](https://www.unmannedtechshop.co.uk/fr/products/geprc-mark-4-spare-arm-5-6-7-inch).

### Inferences
- The user's layout has rear pair 19–20 mm, second pair 20 mm and a wide front pair. A 20 mm middle pair matches the GEPRC pattern (…/20/20/19) better than RJX V2 10" (…/24/24/19).
- The 24 mm inner arm-hole spacing matches neither reference: GEPRC 7" has 20 and 36 mm. This points to a third geometry. Candidates are a GEPRC-style 10" (GEPRC Mark4 10, UDC or other Ukrainian clones), whose arm-bolt pattern may differ from the 7" because the 10" arm is wider; a hybrid clone; or an aftermarket arm cut with its own hole pattern.
- Measuring the outline (about 303–305 × 370–376 mm versus 268 × 332 mm) and motor-to-motor distance (428 versus 427 versus 473 mm) should place the frame in a family quickly. Exact standoff matching still needs the GrabCAD or Cults3D STEP files.

### Gaps
- Arm-bolt spacing for the GEPRC Mark4 10", UDC, FIBERFORM, Tarot, HSKRC and Readytosky frames is not published in any source found.
- Without reading the CAD files, it cannot be confirmed which versions use 20 mm and which use 24 mm middle standoff pairs.

## 4. Are there aftermarket 8 mm replacement arms for Mark4 10" frames?

### Takeaway
Yes, but only one was found. A Ukrainian listing on Rozetka sells a pair of unpainted 8 mm fiberglass (склотекстоліт) arms for the "Mark4 10" V2 427 mm". No 8 mm carbon replacement arm for a Mark4 10" was found from GEPRC or any major clone maker.

### Cited Findings
- Rozetka listing: a pair of arms for the Mark4 10" V2 427 mm frame, fiberglass, unpainted, 8 mm thick, in stock at the time of the snippet — [rozetka.com.ua](https://rozetka.com.ua/ua/459333884/p459333884/)
- GEPRC sells original Mark4 replacement arms (7", 8", 10"), but their thickness and hole patterns were not given in the snippet — [Rotorama](https://www.rotorama.com/product/geprc-mark4-dily)
- A Mark4 7" replacement arm is listed at 5 mm thick — [brookealvinston.com listing](https://brookealvinston.com/products/mark4-7inch-arm-thickness-295mm-5mm-replaceable-accessories/216412196/)
- An 8 mm replacement arm does exist for the Kayou Kayouloin 7", which is not a Mark4 — [Pyrodrone](https://pyrodrone.com/products/kayou-frames-kayouloin-7-inches-replacement-arm-99)
- Ukrainian searches for 8 mm carbon Mark4 10" arms ("промені 8мм карбон", "посилені промені 8мм") found only complete frames with 5–7.5 mm arms — [flyteam.com.ua](https://flyteam.com.ua/frame/mark410v2); [fx-drones.com](https://fx-drones.com/rama-readytosky-mark4-v2-10-427mm-karbonova-rama-10-diuimovoho-fpv-drona/); [ukrob.com.ua](https://ukrob.com.ua/catalog/category-e3af6452/mark4_frame_10ichv2/)

### Inferences
- The Rozetka arms are sold for the "V2 427 mm" frame, so they presumably copy the RJX/Readytosky V2 arm-bolt pattern. If the user's frame has 8 mm fiberglass arms, comparing its arm-bolt spacing with the RJX V2 STEP is a reasonable check. The user's 20 mm middle standoff pair does not match the RJX V2's 24 mm, though, so the arms may be on a different body.
- Other local shops in Ukraine and Eastern Europe may sell similar fiberglass or 8 mm arms that the search engine did not index.

### Gaps
- The Rozetka listing could not be opened, so its arm-bolt spacing, arm length, photos and seller name are unknown.
- No 8 mm carbon aftermarket arm for any Mark4 10" was found.
