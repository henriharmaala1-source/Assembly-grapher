# Marketplace and clone listings: Mark4-style 10" frames with 8 mm arms

Method note (applies to every section): research ran on 2026-10-27 using web-search results only. Direct page fetches (WebFetch and curl) to aliexpress.com, amazon.com, ebay.com, rotorama.com and rcdrone.top all failed in this environment (DNS failure or proxy 403 on CONNECT). So every spec below comes from the search engine's snippet or summary of the listing, not from a page I read myself. Before anyone buys, each figure should be checked against the live page. I could not check stock, sold-out status or current price on any listing. The exceptions are a few prices the search snippets showed. About 15 searches were run, in English and Chinese, covering AliExpress, Amazon, eBay (.com/.de), Banggood, Temu, Walmart, Taobao/1688 and the reseller and store sites they led to.

## Do the general marketplaces (AliExpress, Amazon, Banggood, eBay, Temu, Walmart, Taobao, 1688, JD) list a "Mark4 10 inch 8mm arm" frame?

### Takeaway
I found no marketplace listing for a Mark4-style 10" frame (V2, V3 or V4) that states 8 mm arms, explicitly or by implication. Across all the platforms I could see, the 10" Mark4 clones state 7.5 mm arms (most often), 7 mm (GEPRC original and some clones), 6 mm or 5 mm. In the Mark4 family, 8 mm arms show up only on the larger Readytosky Mark4 V3 Pro 13" (570 mm) and 15" (680 mm) frames. The only 10" frame with 8 mm arms in the results is a non-Mark4 design, the iFlight XL10 V6.

### Inferences
- 427 mm is the de facto wheelbase of the 10" Mark4 V2 clone (the Tarot/Readytosky pattern), and 428–429 mm is the GEPRC original. A figure of 473 mm on Readytosky's text likely comes from transposed digits, since the same listing also says 427 mm. 435 mm did not appear anywhere.
- The "6 mm camera arm" wording on CaptainRC and Jinjiuyi probably comes from a translated spec template. It may describe a camera side plate rather than the motor arms, so do not treat it as an arm-thickness figure without checking.

### Gaps
- Standoff height did not appear in any snippet for any 10" listing.
- Plate thicknesses were given only for Readytosky (2/3 mm), Tarot (3/3 mm), CaptainRC (2/3 mm) and skelbiu.lt (3 mm).
- I could not confirm a store name or price for most AliExpress and Amazon listings.

## Do any listings offer arm-thickness options (e.g., 7 mm / 8 mm selectable)?

### Takeaway
No. None of the listings I found lets the buyer choose arm thickness for the 10" size. The variant selectors are frame size (7/8/9/10"), and the arm thickness follows from the size. The typical pairing is 6 mm arms for 8" and 9" and 7.5 mm arms for 10".

### Cited Findings
- The AliExpress multi-size listing ties thickness to size: "8inch 367mm 9inch 387mm with 6mm Arm / 10inch 427mm w/ 7.5mm Arm". — [AliExpress](https://www.aliexpress.com/item/1005005897270664.html)
- The eBay multi-size listing "MARK4 V2 7/8/9/10" ... 6mm Arm" offers one thickness across all sizes. — [eBay 395595061304](https://www.ebay.com/itm/395595061304)
- The drobonation listing shows a 5.0–7.5 mm arm range and a 427–473 mm wheelbase range across its variants. It does not offer 8 mm. — [drobonation](https://drobonation1.odoo.com/shop/mark4-v2-10-inch-frame-90)

### Inferences
- Listings sold by size can quietly change arm thickness between sizes. A multi-size listing's headline thickness (for example eBay's "6mm Arm") may not hold for the 10" option.

### Gaps
- I could not inspect the variant drop-downs on AliExpress, Amazon or eBay, because the pages were unreachable.

## Are there replacement 8 mm arms for Mark4 10" frames sold separately?

### Takeaway
I found no 8 mm replacement arm for a 10" Mark4. Separate 10" Mark4 arms do exist, at 7 mm (AOCODA) and as GEPRC Mark4 10" spares (Rotorama). The 8 mm arms I found belong to the 13"/15" V3 Pro frames, which are larger.

### Cited Findings
- AOCODA-RC: "Mark 4 10inch 420mm Arm Thickness 7mm for Mark4 FPV Racing Drone Quadcopter Freestyle Frame Kit", listed in stock. Hole pattern not given in the snippet. — [AOCODA-RC](https://www.aocoda-rc.com/products/72)
- Rotorama: "GEPRC Mark4 - replacement arms" in 7", 8" and 10" sizes, €7.79 per piece, listed in stock. These fit the GEPRC original, which has 7 mm arms. — [Rotorama](https://www.rotorama.com/product/geprc-mark4-dily)
- Pyrodrone: GEP-RC Mark4 replacement 7" arm (1 pc). This is the wrong size for a 10". — [Pyrodrone](https://pyrodrone.com/products/gep-rc-mark4-replacement-7-arm-1-pc)
- A Mark4 7" 295 mm arm, 5 mm thick, is listed as a replaceable accessory (wrong size, and the site is of doubtful standing). — [brookealvinston.com](https://brookealvinston.com/products/mark4-7inch-arm-thickness-295mm-5mm-replaceable-accessories/216412196/)
- The Amazon "Replacement for MARK4 V2 10inch Frame 425mm 1PC" is a complete frame, not separate arms. The search summary gives 7.0 mm arms. — [Amazon B0GGMDRL9F](https://www.amazon.com/Replacement-10inch-Freestyle-Compatible-Propeller/dp/B0GGMDRL9F)

### Inferences
- A builder who wants 8 mm arms on a 10" Mark4 would probably have to get them custom cut from the open Mark4 10" CAD/STEP files, or adapt V3 Pro arms. I found no evidence that either is sold. — CAD reference: [Cults3D Mark 4 10 inch STEP files](https://cults3d.com/en/3d-model/gadget/frame-mark-4-10-inch-step-cad-files)

### Gaps
- No arm-mounting hole pattern was given in any snippet for the 10" replacement arms (AOCODA, Rotorama).
- None of the arm listings' stock status or prices could be checked live.
