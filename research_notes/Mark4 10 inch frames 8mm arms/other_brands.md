# Non-Mark4-branded 10-inch (9-10" long range) FPV frames with 8 mm carbon arms, and how Mark4-like each is

> **Method note (read first).** In this session, direct page fetching (WebFetch/curl) was blocked by the sandbox egress proxy. getfpv.com, hglrc.com, shop.iflight.com, rcdrone.top, oscarliang.com and rotorama.com all failed with "ENOTFOUND" or "CONNECT 403". So every finding below comes from the **WebSearch result summaries and snippets** for the URLs shown, not from a full read of each page. Where a summary quoted spec-line text (for example "Arm Thickness: 8mm"), I give it as quoted. Treat figures as "as listed in search snippets" and check them on the page before relying on them. Prices and stock dates are what the search snippets reported in October 2026.
>
> **Scope.** Only standard hobby FPV frames are covered. No weaponised or payload-drop frames were found or included.

## Which 10" frames list 8 mm arms?

### Takeaway
Only a few current non-Mark4 10" quad frames clearly list 8 mm arms:
- **iFlight Helion 10** (418 mm). The 8 mm figure is consistent across sources and matches iFlight's own spec line.
- **HGLRC / Rekon FPV Rekon10 Pro**: V1 is 455 mm, V2 is 474 mm. Both list 8 mm front and rear arms.
- **iFlight XL10 V6** (420 mm). The arm figure is disputed: 8 mm on rcdrone.top, Banggood, ReadyMadeRC and Made-in-China listings, 7 mm on GetFPV, NextFPV and the iFlight shop page.

Most other 10" frames checked list 7 mm arms (Foxeer Aura 10, Axisflying MANTA10, GEPRC Pulsar LR10, DarwinFPV X10, JeeFly XL10 V7, QWinOut LX10 V2) or 6 mm (iFlight Chimera CX10, QWinOut APEX EVO 10). For the other brands searched, no 10" model with 8 mm arms turned up.

## Which have a Mark4-like layout vs a different one?

### Takeaway
None of the 8 mm candidates is a straight Mark4 copy, but **Rekon10 Pro (V1/V2)** and **XL10 V6** are the closest structurally. Both are listed with a three-plate stack (bottom, middle/"upper", top) that sandwiches separate arms, use a 30.5×30.5 FC mount, and use eight aluminium standoffs. Their geometry differs: Rekon is stretched (455–474 mm, separate front and rear arm shapes, 20 mm top-to-middle gap), and the XL10 is True-X with 25 mm and 35 mm standoffs. **Helion 10** is the least Mark4-like. It is listed with only bottom and upper plates (4 mm), CNC camera side plates and an "A-Frame" body, so it is a heavier, enclosed design.

### Cited Findings
- Rekon10 Pro V1: three plates (top 3 / middle 2 / bottom 3 mm), "top and middle plate space: 20mm", FC 30.5×30.5, and eight aluminium standoffs (2× 30 mm, 4× 20 mm, 2× 18 mm). — [GetFPV](https://www.getfpv.com/rekon-fpv-rekon10-pro-long-range-frame-kit-10.html); [Rising Sun FPV](https://risingsunfpv.com/products/rekon10-pro-long-range-frame-kit-10-inch-for-fpv-racing-drone)
- Rekon10 Pro V2: three plates (top 2 / middle 3 / bottom 3 mm), separate front and rear V2 arm spares, FC 30.5 or 20×20 M3, and a "triangular reinforcement structure". — [HGLRC](https://www.hglrc.com/products/hglrc-rekon10-pro-v2-10-inch-long-range-frame-compatible-with-o4); [RDQ front arm V2](https://www.racedayquads.com/products/hglrc-rekon10-pro-front-arm-v2)
