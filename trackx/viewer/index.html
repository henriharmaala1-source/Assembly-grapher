Taken from the sources

  • Driver and commander sit at the front behind a large armoured windscreen.
  • Two side doors for the crew and a rear door hinged on the left for the ten dismounts.
  • A forward-opening commander's roof hatch.
  • Drive sprocket at the front, idler at the rear, six dual rubber-tyred road wheels and two return rollers a side.
  • 56 cm rubber tracks and an almost flat underside.

My estimates

  • Windscreen rake and size, bonnet height and the angle of the front plate.
  • Door, window and hatch sizes and positions.
  • Wheel, sprocket and idler diameters, and the track cleat pattern.
  • Colour. The photos and the makers' pages could not be opened, so nothing here was traced from a picture.

Left off

  • The roof-mounted weapon station and the pintle gun.
  • The drone jammer, antennas and mirrors.
  • Interior, engine and transmission.
